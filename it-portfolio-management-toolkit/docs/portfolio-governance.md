# Portfolio Governance – Reference Model

## Suggested cadence
- Weekly workstream review: delivery progress, blockers, dependencies, milestones.
- Biweekly portfolio review: capacity, cross-project conflicts, risk exposure, priorities.
- Monthly steering committee: outcomes, investment decisions, scope trade-offs, escalations.

## Minimum controls
1. Named business and technology owners.
2. Approved scope and measurable outcomes.
3. Baseline milestones and forecast dates.
4. RAID log with owners and due dates.
5. Change control for material scope or timeline changes.
6. Decision log and action tracking.

## RAG definitions
- Green: within agreed tolerance; no unresolved critical blocker.
- Amber: material risk threatens a milestone or outcome; mitigation is active.
- Red: committed outcome is unlikely without intervention or a decision.

Adapt thresholds to the organization's governance model.
